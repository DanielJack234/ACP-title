Watermelon is a highly hydrating, nutrient-dense fruit (92% water) belonging to the Cucurbitaceae family, technically classified as a pepo berry with a thick, hard rind and sweet flesh. It is rich in vitamins A and C, potassium, magnesium, and the antioxidant lycopene. Low in calories (roughly 30-46 kcal per 100g), it supports cardiovascular health, hydration, and reduces inflammation.
